## Limits

This is an automated regression and source review, not proof that every production/device scenario works. An actual iPhone camera, permission prompts, email delivery and the operating-system share/print sheet require real-device verification. No comparative speed or penetration-test certification is claimed.

Local caches and pending receipts remain without app-level encryption. App Check, MFA, automatic bank payment verification and a trusted financial-validation endpoint are not implemented. See security/SECURITY.md for the full security scope.

Keep testing on the existing scan-direct origin. Other preview origins may contain unsynchronized device-only receipts and were not deleted. Query-string release markers do not create new Hosting channels.
